# FloodGuard

## Environmental Risk Awareness Platform

### Hackathon Track
Heat and Water

### Project Goal

FloodGuard is a lightweight environmental risk-awareness platform that converts rainfall and water-level observations into simple, location-based flood and waterlogging risk indicators and safety recommendations.

---

## 1. Problem

During heavy rainfall, people may know that it is raining, but they may not know:

- Whether their area is becoming risky
- Whether water levels are increasing
- Whether they should avoid low-lying roads
- Whether travelling is safe
- Which nearby area has higher risk

FloodGuard aims to make environmental information easier to understand and act upon.

---

## 2. Target Users

- Students
- Commuters
- Local residents
- Colleges

---

## 3. Input Data

FloodGuard initially uses:

- Location
- Rainfall
- Water level
- Updated time

Initial demo locations:

- Trichy
- Thanjavur
- Salem

---

## 4. Risk Levels

### 🟢 SAFE

Low environmental risk.

Recommendation:
Normal travel with awareness.

### 🟡 WARNING

Moderate environmental risk.

Recommendation:
Be cautious around low-lying areas.

### 🔴 HIGH

High environmental risk.

Recommendation:
Avoid low-lying and potentially waterlogged roads.

---

## 5. Core User Flow

Location Selection
        ↓
Environmental Data
        ↓
Risk Engine
        ↓
Risk Level
        ↓
Safety Recommendation

---

## 6. Example

Location: Trichy

Rainfall: 85 mm

Water Level: 70 cm

Risk: 🔴 HIGH

Recommendation:
Avoid low-lying roads.

---

## 7. Important Limitation

FloodGuard is an environmental awareness and risk-indicator system.

It is not an official flood-warning system and does not claim to provide scientific flood prediction.

The initial prototype uses configurable project-defined thresholds.
